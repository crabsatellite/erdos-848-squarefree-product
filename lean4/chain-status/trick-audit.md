## Rule Meanings

- `I1.forbidden-axiom`: endpoint-reached axiom not whitelisted.
- `I4.assumption-as-goal`: theorem has a premise syntactically identical to its conclusion.
- `I5.vacuous-prop-def`: on-chain `def : Prop` is a literal True/False/Unit-style placeholder.
- `I6.vacuous-theorem`: on-chain theorem conclusion is literally True/Unit-style.
- `W5.prop-def`: audit-visible `def : Prop`; check it is definitional infrastructure.
- `W5.suspicious-prop-def`: `def : Prop` name suggests hypothesis/strengthening/placeholder risk.
- `W6.vacuous-theorem`: off-chain theorem with a literal True/Unit-style conclusion.
